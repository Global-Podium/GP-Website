Where We've Been

Past events.

Model UN 2024

GPMUN

Our journey began with GPMUN, held on 13 & 14 April 2024. It brought young voices together through diplomacy, debate, and conversations on global issues.

Model UN 2024

GPMUN 2.0

On 18 & 19 May 2024, we returned with GPMUN 2.0. Building on our first experience, the event created another space for young delegates to exchange ideas, debate, and learn from one another.

Youth Summit 2024

GP Youth Summit

The GP Youth Summit was held on 15 & 16 June 2024. It brought together young minds to explore leadership, share perspectives, and engage in conversations about the future.

Workshop 2024

Leadership Workshop

On 24 May 2024, we conducted a Leadership Workshop at GSJMS School, Jodhpur. The session focused on building confidence, leadership skills, communication, and collaboration among students.

Model UN 2024

GPMUN 3.0

GPMUN 3.0 took place on 21 & 22 July 2024. Continuing our MUN journey, it gave young delegates another opportunity to step into diplomacy, challenge perspectives, and make their voices heard.

Orientation 2024

Orientation Workshop

On 12 July 2024, we held an orientation workshop at CMS Aliganj. The session introduced students to the world of Model United Nations, diplomacy, and confident public speaking.

Model UN 2024

Gurukul MUN

Gurukul MUN was held on 7 & 8 September 2024 as an offline gathering. It brought students together in person for two days of debate, diplomacy, learning, and meaningful connections.

Christmas Celebration 2024

Christmas Celebration

On 25 December 2024, we celebrated Christmas with the children of Poornima Paathshala. The offline celebration was filled with joy, laughter, togetherness, and the simple happiness of sharing the festive spirit.

Model UN 2025

IIT Roorkee MUN

From 17–19 January 2025, Global Podium was proud to be one of the partners for the IIT Roorkee MUN. Being part of the event gave us the opportunity to support and connect with a community of young minds brought together by their passion for diplomacy and meaningful dialogue.

Model UN 2025

GPSMUN

GPSMUN 2025 was held offline on 4 & 5 October 2025. Bringing young delegates together in person, the event created a space for thoughtful debate, new perspectives, and lasting connections.
