Our Story

And this is where
we began.

Global Podium began with a simple idea: create a space where young people could meet, speak, learn, and be heard. What began with conversations and events grew into a community of curious minds. Today, Global Podium continues to grow across events, media, talent, and technology — but the heart of it remains the same: bringing people together and creating something meaningful along the way.

What We Stand For

Four commitments behind everything we run.

People first

We believe good things begin with people. We create spaces where young voices, creators, and fresh ideas can be heard.

Make it meaningful

Whether it’s an event, a story, a collaboration, or a new idea, we want everything we build to have a reason behind it.

Grow from where we are

Global Podium started small, and that spirit stays with us. We value our roots while continuing to reach new people and places.

Always building

There’s always something new around the corner. From events to artists, media, and technology, we’re here to keep creating and growing.

Our Journey

From meaningful conversations to a growing community

2024

Where it all began

Global Podium began its journey in 2024, bringing young voices together through various conferences, debates and events. We also took our work offline through Gurukul MUN, creating spaces for dialogue, learning, and community.

2025

Growing through collaboration

In 2025, Global Podium continued to grow through new events and collaborations, including our partnership with IIT Roorkee for its MUN, Unity X GPMUN, and GPSMUN 2025. The year strengthened our community and expanded our vision beyond individual events.

2026

Building what comes next

In 2026, Global Podium began shaping a broader ecosystem, extending our work beyond events into new spaces through Global Podium Artists Agency, Global Podium Media Agency, and Global Podium Technologies — each built around our belief in people, ideas, creativity, and innovation.

2026

Jaipur Global Heritage Summit

Our flagship international summit returns home to Jaipur, pairing global dialogue with the city that started it all.
